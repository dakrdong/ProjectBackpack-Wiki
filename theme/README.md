# PackBound wiki theme image contract

These raster assets give the documentation shell a PackBound identity without
turning the reading surface into an illustration gallery. Keep art inside the
three deliberate slots below; page-authored evidence remains in
`wiki/content/media`.

| Asset | Render slot | Export | Crop contract |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `packbound-wiki-hero.webp` | Tree and article hero | 2172×724, 3:1 | Dark negative space on the left; characters and world landmarks in the middle/right; mobile may crop to the female rookie and backpack charms. |
| `packbound-sidebar-vignette.webp` | Fixed sidebar footer | 940×418 | Copy-safe left half and the Scottish Fold charm on the right. |
| `packbound-brand-emblem.webp` | Topbar identity mark | 256×256 | Backpack and charm must remain legible at 34–36 CSS pixels. |

The approved charm is a golden-yellow chubby Scottish Fold with low
forward-folded ears, round cheeks, half-lidded unimpressed eyes, a tiny sour
mouth, and a coral collar. It should feel aloof and mildly nasty at first glance
but cute on a second look. Do not substitute upright triangular ears, a dog-like
muzzle, or the male rookie's duck charm.

When regenerating, preserve the dark navy, electric purple, acid-lime, coral,
cyan, and warm orange palette; chunky toy-like volumes; thick deep-purple
outlines; and the Junkwind Meadow landmarks. Export a new source version for any
design change, then intentionally replace these web delivery derivatives and
verify both desktop and phone crops in a browser.
